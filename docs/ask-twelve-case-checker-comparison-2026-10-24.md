# Twelve Arabic source checks: neither checker earns activation or replacement

## Plain-English result

The current checker accepts faithful paraphrases but still lets several changed meanings through. Its configured backup blocks those statements, but also rejects faithful statements and sometimes gives reasons contradicted by the supplied source. Switching to the backup, or requiring both models to agree, is **not an accepted accuracy repair**.

The comparison cost **$0.00662064375, about 0.66 cents**, within Mo's approved five-cent maximum. No website model, privacy setting, evidence boundary or answer-generation behavior changed.

## Scope

Mo explicitly approved “Compare within 5 cents” using existing evaluation funds. Twelve fixed Arabic statements were evaluated once by each existing checker: 24 settled generations, not 24 visitor questions. The bank contains six source-supported controls and six defective controls, covering scope, conditions, causes and invented outcomes. The disputed non-Muslim-parent application remains excluded and unscored.

The bank and expected labels were fixed before dispatch. Each pair uses the same prepared single-claim narrow-check request and its own exact originals/restrictive context. Expected labels and review reasons never enter the provider request. The same pinned current models, ZDR/data-collection-denied settings and strict output contracts are retained. Mistral keeps its already-approved omission of an unsupported optional reasoning parameter; there is no new compatibility change.

These selected regressions are not held-out questions, qualified scholarly certification or a general religious-accuracy benchmark. Counts below concern decision agreement with those fixed source-support labels. An aligned negative decision does not establish that its explanation diagnoses the actual defect.

## What the errors mean

### Current checker

It again approves four defective statements:

- S01: expands the own verse's gratitude benefit into worship and adds a cause.
- S04: drops belief from the reward recipients and adds dwellings/stations.
- S09: accepts the new disjunction that turns a phrase within pressure toward association into a separate no-knowledge exception.
- S11: accepts practical ties/assistance advice with its explicit source qualification omitted.

It correctly rejects the invented wisdom/pardon cause and guaranteed plentiful provision outcome. Accepting all six faithful controls is useful evidence about unnecessary refusal, but it does not offset the four false approvals.

### Backup checker

The four false rejections concern statements whose relevant meanings are in their own originals: belief **and** good deeds for reward, the hadith's non-association condition, no requested provision/feeding, and gratitude benefiting the person who gives thanks.

Several negative decisions on defective statements also have incorrect explanations:

- S04: it claims bounty/reward is not explicit, although the own 30:45 contains “من فضله”. That is not the actual omitted-condition/added-outcome defect.
- S09: it disputes command versus instruction and prohibition versus “do not obey” wording in the supported core. It does not identify the changed disjunction.
- S11: it says visits are absent, although “ويزورهما” is explicit. It does not identify the missing rejection/expulsion qualification.

S01's explanation is imprecise about the combination of worship and gratitude; gratitude's personal benefit is explicit and must not be described as absent. The backup gives appropriate reasons for rejecting the invented wisdom/pardon cause and provision guarantee. Its two new faithful parent-related controls pass, but this does not make its incorrect critiques reliable.

Those critiques must remain untrusted feedback. Feeding them directly to the writer could remove a supported point rather than restore the missing condition. A unanimous-approval rule would block all six defective controls while also withholding four faithful ones. It has not been activated.

Missing execution caps preserve existing behavior; invalid caps fail. The guard snapshots the cap for its lifetime. A separately authorized future continuation must not silently inherit this finished task's limit or reset the overall allowance.

The runner checks live endpoint prices against existing ceilings before paid calls and applies matching provider price ceilings. It stops after a provider rejection, unreadable result or uncertain charge; there was no retry, fallback chain, partial salvage or added model.
