# New to Islam: ordered content review

19 October 2026. Ordered content and source review, all 32 lessons and three languages. Editorial review, not independent scholarly approval.

Reviewed in order: 32 of 32. Length and passing software checks are not treated as proof of religious or linguistic accuracy.

## 1. What is Islam?

**Finding:** The outline was relevant, but much of the core and deeper explanation described the course instead of explaining Islam; the conversion follow-up omitted acting on the commitment.

**Change:** Rewrote all three versions with a direct definition, explained pillar names, connected belief/practice/character using direct passages, added concrete everyday examples and restored the full attributed conversion qualifications.

**Checked sources:** [https://quran.com/4/125](https://quran.com/4/125), [https://quran.com/4/36](https://quran.com/4/36), [https://quran.com/2/177](https://quran.com/2/177), [https://sunnah.com/muslim:8a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:8a), [https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/1263/الامور-التي-يدخل-بها-في-الاسلام](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/1263/الامور-التي-يدخل-بها-في-الاسلام)

**Languages:** English, Arabic and German compared for the same beliefs, ability condition, examples, attribution and follow-ups.



## 2. Who is Allah?

**Finding:** The answer explained uniqueness and independence but said little positively about Allah, and the deeper section mostly repeated the core.

**Change:** Added Quran-supported knowledge, mercy, hearing and seeing; explained three central meanings with concrete implications; clarified that the origin FAQ states the belief rather than claiming to finish a philosophical debate.

**Checked sources:** [https://quran.com/112](https://quran.com/112), [https://quran.com/35/15](https://quran.com/35/15), [https://quran.com/39/62](https://quran.com/39/62), [https://quran.com/59/22](https://quran.com/59/22), [https://quran.com/42/11](https://quran.com/42/11), [https://sunnah.com/muslim:2713a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2713a)

**Languages:** English, Arabic and German compared for the same attributes, Creator/creation distinction and limits of the origin answer.



## 3. Why are we here, and what does worship mean?

**Finding:** The purpose answer was supported but underexplained what worship means and how intention relates to the action; the deeper section mainly repeated the family example.

**Change:** Connected worship to the Prophet's whole-life declaration, explained accountability and the need for a religious act to follow the teaching, and expanded the prayer-only follow-up without claiming all activities automatically become worship.

**Checked sources:** [https://quran.com/51/56](https://quran.com/51/56), [https://quran.com/67/2](https://quran.com/67/2), [https://quran.com/6/162](https://quran.com/6/162), [https://quran.com/99/6-8](https://quran.com/99/6-8), [https://sunnah.com/bukhari:56](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:56), [https://sunnah.com/muslim:1718b](https://sunnah.com/muslim:1718b)
