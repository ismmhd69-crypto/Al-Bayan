## What remains clearly wrong

There are demonstrable source-faithfulness errors: adding a separate exception to a Quran condition, dropping an explicitly stated recipient qualification, and attributing causes or promises that the cited passage does not establish. These remain errors even if a statement sounds familiar or could have support somewhere else.

There are also clear false rejections. A faithful paraphrase does not need to repeat the source's exact grammatical form. For example, the no-feeding statement in the test follows the wording of [51:57](https://quran.com/ar/adh-dhariyat/57); treating a noun-versus-verb difference as missing meaning is not a sound rejection.

## Corrections to my earlier framing

### 1. Literal source support and a safe public explanation are different questions

S03 reproduces a meaning stated in the narration, so its narrow matn-only label has a textual basis. The full source page nevertheless gives relevant interpretive context. I should have marked that distinction before treating this example as a clean religious-accuracy control. The backup did not identify that published contextual point specifically; its vague rejection is not upgraded to expert reasoning after the fact. [Published hadith and explanation](https://hadeethenc.com/ar/browse/hadith/65007).

This does not erase the recorded outputs or retroactively improve either model. It means the original comparison measured agreement with fixed source-support labels, not certified truth across all public religious explanations.

### 2. Missing a practical qualification is not the same as inventing a ruling

The wording about visits and assistance needs the source's practical qualification when giving a complete answer about continuing those acts. The stronger English version requiring “ongoing support” especially needs careful limits. But I should not imply that refusal of contact ends every duty of kindness. Another [Ibn Baz answer](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/2799/هل-يجب-على-الابن-الدعاء-لوالده-بعد-وفاته) explicitly says parental rejection/expulsion does not itself eliminate dutifulness and prayer for the parent. These are different acts and contexts; this review does not turn one excerpt's qualification into a blanket rule about all kindness.

### 3. The non-Muslim-parent summary has substantive support

The earlier final checker withheld a statement about kindness to non-Muslim parents because the exact religious-status label was not explicit in the supplied verse wording. Reopening the original scholarship resolves the broader interpretive question: [Ibn Baz 8881](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/8881/الضابط-في-طاعة-الوالدين) expressly applies 31:15 to non-Muslim parents while retaining limits on obedience. An [additional official explanation](https://binbaz.org.sa/audios/1015/ضرورة-طاعة-الوالدين-والاحسان-اليهما-وان-كانا-كافرين) also addresses that situation directly.

My assessment is that this general parents application should not be called religiously wrong merely because the word “non-Muslim” is absent from the verse's surface wording. The sources found now do **not** become retroactive citations for the earlier generated sentence. Future output still needs the evidence actually available in its sealed package, appropriate attribution and relevant qualifications. No newly found page has been imported or auto-approved for the website.

The same official material also supplies capacity/harm or financial-need qualifications. Those must not disappear if an answer goes further into practical or financial demands. [Ibn Baz's detailed limits](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/8881/الضابط-في-طاعة-الوالدين).

## What the comparison can fairly establish

- The historical fixed-bank decisions remain exactly as recorded: the primary approves four of six labelled-defective controls; the backup rejects four of six labelled-faithful controls.
- Those figures must not be presented as an Islamic accuracy score. S03 has a textual-versus-explanatory-context distinction, and S11 concerns completeness of practical advice rather than an automatically false doctrine.
- Even setting those nuanced cases aside, the primary still approves clear scope/cause failures, while the backup still rejects clearly faithful belief/reward, provision/feeding and gratitude paraphrases. The case for an automatic model switch or unanimous vote is not established.
- The current wording and checking problem includes both over-extension and overly literal refusal. Lowering all checks or demanding exact keyword repetition would not solve both.

## Concrete next direction with current models

1. Keep source support, completeness/qualification and interpretation/context as separate review outcomes.
2. Treat genuine paraphrases by meaning. Treat a changed conditional relationship, lost required recipient or invented promise as a substantive error.
3. For narrations needing interpretive context, retrieve an appropriate approved explanation rather than pretending the isolated wording settles every implication.
4. For a case-specific application, prefer directly relevant named scholarly evidence and preserve its limits. Do not use a broad rule plus a topical keyword to invent a personal ruling.
5. Revise future control labels transparently where interpretation or completeness is involved. Preserve this paid comparison's original inputs/results and separate any later bank version.
