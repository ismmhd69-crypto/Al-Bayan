# Ask meaning and answer-quality repair

Implemented locally on 24 October 2026. No paid model calls or deployment.

## Changes

- The writer and active rich checker share meaning-preservation rules: audience, recipients, nested conditions, exceptions, causes and strength. Faithful paraphrases need no identical keyword. A short supported teaching is distinct from incomplete practical advice.
- Primary and narrow negative findings identify affected claim wording, the actual source ID and an exact source quotation. The server checks both quotations against the supplied claim and its own originals or attached restrictive context. Borrowed unrelated sources, invented quotations and missing receipts are checker failures. A valid quotation does not establish that the semantic judgment is correct.
- Internal outcomes distinguish source support, missing qualifications, context needs, question coverage and checker failure. Checked sections survive later malformed checking, blanket unsupported safety verdicts and editorial complaints. Grounded findings against an earlier claim still trigger withdrawal. No new draft is approved from a broken checker response.
- Corrections retain the existing bounded allowance and identity-locked replacement mechanism. Grounded negative feedback is passed to the correction; normal checks run again. No voting, extra checking layer, experimental feature activation or new retry loop.
- Rich evidence carries optional source-identified Arabic context with location, relationship and citation eligibility. Neighbouring verses remain restrictive context unless independently selected. Explicit internal links may carry an already-selected named scholar's explanation. The context cannot approve a source, add a citation automatically or fill missing support.
- Missing explanatory context is marked explicitly. HadeethEnc's unnamed commentary remains excluded under the existing approved-scholars rule. This repair does not automatically discover/import new commentary or create durable storage. A required interpretation still needs eligible evidence in the actual answer package.
- Technical interruptions have a separate Arabic, English and German visitor message. Existing empty-group hiding, single-group completion, section order and incomplete-answer saving rules remain covered by tests. Patient-worker activation and durable recovery remain gated.
