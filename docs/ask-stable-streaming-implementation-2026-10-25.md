## Publication contract and backend review

- `lib/ask/publication.ts` owns immutable commits, evidence snapshot hashes, exact duplicate handling, targeted integrity notices and the reading stages.
- Stream v3 binds every event to its request and sequence. A conflicting commit is a protocol error. Ordinary progress cannot clear a commit or restart the answer at understanding.
- `lib/ask/stable-lanes.ts` keeps candidates and drafts private. Each group completes selection, permitted recovery, correction and checks before publication. Research can overlap; display is buffered in Quran, Sunnah, scholar, conclusion order.
- Whole-question coverage checks only the union of originals actually cited by committed sections. A new ranking cannot replace those sections. Final writing is limited to the conclusion.
- Continue replays unchanged commits after rechecking their originals. Unavailable rechecking withholds completion without inventing a finding against the earlier text. Confirmed changed originals produce a targeted notice.
- With the rollout flag enabled, the existing patient coordinator uses stable commits in the real pipeline. Its final review cannot rewrite published source sections or recover new evidence for them. Its existing v2 wire format remains readable; an optional integrity field gives the new reader a precise rejection target. Old saved answer formats remain supported. Flag-off behaviour is tested separately.
- Both transports share the publication ledger. Duplicate patient section delivery is harmless when its commit identity and checked content match. Conflicting duplicates fail closed.
- Stable v3 installs an unlimited application research runtime, including source and correction operations, independently of the local debugging flag. The remaining scholar search race follows this policy too. Stop and existing cumulative spending, call and token controls remain. External provider and hosting limits cannot be removed by a browser request.
