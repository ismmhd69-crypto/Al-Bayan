### Displayed Quran explanation 1: unsupported added scope

The sentence ends with “على الشرك أو ما لا علم له به”. Its own source is 29:8. In that source, “ما ليس لك به علم” occurs within the pressure to associate partners with God. The generated **أو** creates a separate alternative: something the child lacks knowledge of. That broader exception is not established by the cited verse.

The kindness instruction and refusal under pressure toward association have support. The added alternative does not. Primary and narrow meaning checks approved it both initially and in later reviews. The final result leaves the sentence visible. This is a genuine source-faithfulness failure, despite valid citations and complete checker output.

### Displayed Quran explanation 2: supported

The cited 31:15 explicitly joins worldly companionship in kindness with refusal of pressure toward association. This explanation preserves that setting and does not demand unconditional obedience.

### Displayed Sunnah explanation: supported

The own Sahihayn narration HE66184 describes a commander's demand that people enter a fire and ends with the restriction “الطاعة في المعروف”. The explanation attributes the restriction of obedience to the Prophet and preserves its meaning. It does not decide an individual visitor's case.

Finding this narration is useful live retrieval evidence. It does not prove that the earlier title-catalogue miss, or source recall generally, is fixed.

### Unshown scholar explanations: useful cores, unresolved qualification and repetition

Another lists kindness, visits and assistance while dropping the excerpt's qualification when parents expel the child and reject those ties. This time it says “ينبغي” rather than inventing an explicit obligation, but the qualification is still absent.
