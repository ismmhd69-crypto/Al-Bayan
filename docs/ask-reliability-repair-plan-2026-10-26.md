# Ask reliability repair plan

Date: 26 October 2026

**Status: implementation authorized by Mo on 26 October 2026; in progress.** See [the implementation record](ask-reliability-implementation-2026-10-26.md). The goal is the complete plan, including useful live answers. Existing spending limits and deployment boundaries remain.

**Scope history:** the report-strengthening revision changed this Markdown plan only. Mo subsequently resumed implementation under the active “implement it” goal. Existing progress is not proof that the remaining gates have passed; the original spending and deployment boundaries remain.

The earlier read-only investigation is recorded in [the investigation findings](ask-reliability-investigation-2026-10-26.md). Subsequent implementation and verification are tracked separately; a planned phase is not complete merely because its first repair passes tests.

Strengthened with the useful points from the subsequent reports: candidate retention through ranking, complementary and cross-facet contributions, correction resilience, preserved useful explanations, and measured output allowances. The seven phases and scope remain unchanged. Estimated percentage improvements are not acceptance evidence.

## Outcome

Give the reader a useful explanation grounded in approved sources. Relevant passages may contribute different parts of an answer. A passage does not need to answer the whole question by itself.

Preserve the reading order: **Quran → Sunnah → approved scholars → Bayan's conclusion.** Missing optional material from one source group must not invalidate an otherwise supported answer. Missing necessary evidence must remain visible as a limitation, and must prevent an unsupported conclusion.

The product goal remains a written answer with citations. A page of source cards alone is not successful acceptance.

## Boundaries

- English and Arabic only. German remains postponed; preserve its existing assets.
- Keep Haiku 5.5 as the primary writer and checker and preserve the configured backups during this repair. Record which model actually answers each stage.
- Keep source identity, authenticity, context, scope, conditions, attribution and claim-support checks. Unknown citations and invented religious assertions must still fail.
- Keep application research deadlines disabled. Preserve Stop, spending protection and aggregate call/output limits. Hosted execution is a separate deployment concern.
- Preserve immutable published sections, stable stream identities and targeted integrity notices. Do not replace the streaming architecture again.
- No new religious-source integrations, migrations, deployment, commits or pushes are included.
- Do not add topic-specific exceptions or hardcoded successful answers to make the examples pass.
- Do not expand a spending allowance, clear an unknown receipt or assume a rejected request was free. Inspect the existing operator ledgers before any later live evaluation.
- Do not add visitor questions, conversation text or source originals to general diagnostics. Existing private traces stay private; shared reports use IDs and reason codes.

### Fresh inventory checkpoint

Read-only metadata on 26 October found **14,629 published hadith sources and 7,255 published fatwa sources** in the Bayan database. There are 7,388 fatwa records in total. Approved search-document rows joined to published sources number 14,629 for hadith and 7,255 for fatwas.

These are metadata counts, not a guarantee that every source is eligible, uniquely indexed, context-complete or retrievable. The source-rights gates and actual search quality remain to be verified. Gemini's approximate 300-fatwa inventory is not a valid basis for this plan. The active title-search route and disabled broader-corpus flag matter independently of how much data is stored.

### Corrections to the external investigations

Use the reports as leads, not as approved implementation specifications:

- Current code already supports complementary direct contributions and explicitly linked background. Do not claim every passage must independently answer the entire question. Trace why the existing paths fail.
- `related_requirement_ids` means relevance to a question part. It is not proof that the passage answers that part. Never automatically copy it into direct coverage.
- Current combined coverage can withhold a conclusion while preserving committed sections. Do not implement this existing behavior again.
- Exact committed-claim meaning approvals already have reuse rules. New summary claims still require review in their actual role and context.
- Ordinary attribution such as “the scholar explained that” is already allowed. Identify the exact forbidden pattern before changing quotation checks.
- A copying rejection does not establish a false positive. Inspect the actual overlap. Lowering an overlap threshold from 60% to 50% would reject more writing.
- Search query filtering drops whole queries that lack meaningful terms; it does not simply strip words from every query. Demonstrate harmful filtering with a real saved input before altering it.
- In the saved 16-candidate Quran selection, removing Q4:174 leaves zero eligible direct contributions and zero eligible linked-background entries under those same assessments. Candidate-local rejection is worth repairing, but does not by itself create an answer in this example.
- Background did reach the hadith writer in the Islam-truth trace. Its package explicitly said the group did not need to answer every concern. Investigate the actual later support/usefulness failure rather than treating this as universal exclusion of context.
- Existing stored search routes, optional corpus retrieval, mapped scholar pages and conditional live recovery are different capabilities. Do not describe the whole searchable system using one route's limits or assume an enabled service was invoked.
- Corrections are already bounded. Do not add a new two-retry policy on the assumption that the current system retries indefinitely. Inspect the active branch and its existing shared limits first.
- Keep request IDs, source IDs, language and event sequence attached to every reported failure. The latest report mixes Arabic-parents outcomes with a different promises run. Do not use those combined timings or its conflicting source identities as a baseline.
- A `failed_check` code identifies a rejected draft, not whether the checker was too strict. A `no_direct_evidence` result describes that attempt, not the absence of suitable evidence from the entire collection. Neither diagnosis justifies lowering support standards by itself.

## Phase 0: establish a trustworthy baseline

**Purpose:** identify the first point where each failed answer loses useful material.

1. Verify the current active path, flags, role chains, budgets and source inventory against the code. Older documentation is historical evidence, not current configuration proof.
2. Read the saved requests and provider responses listed below. Distinguish the model's classification from the application's decision.
3. Build an offline failure table for each case: candidate counts by source family, selected direct contributions, retained background, first exclusion reason, draft failures, correction changes, approved claims, terminal reason and actual provider use.
4. Select a small reference set before implementation. Include the three recorded questions, a multi-part question needing complementary passages, a question with related but insufficient context, an unrelated-source control and an unsupported-condition control. Keep language variants paired where practical.
5. Label expected passage contributions against the originals. Do not label a failed claim “safe” solely because one model approved it.
6. Preserve the fresh inventory checkpoint, but verify source-rights eligibility, family routing, excerpt availability and deployed search behavior separately. Trace each limit between stored data and writer input. The deeper investigation has completed this first routing/count inspection; it has not completed the labeled reference set or semantic review.
7. Maintain one private candidate-retention worksheet per reference case: source ID/family and original hash; eligibility; discovery route; ranking score and retained/dropped decision; selection role and rejection reason; package inclusion; supported claim IDs; and final publication. Bind every score to the exact candidate order. Distinguish the initial search from recovery and the actual model from its configured primary. This prevents attributing a selection failure to ranking or combining timings from different attempts.
8. Preserve a small existing successful sample alongside the failed cases. Pair proposed improvements with controls for unrelated material, missing conditions and unsupported synthesis. Record source/configuration versions so a changed corpus or route is not mistaken for a parser improvement. Do not relabel a failed answerable case as a justified refusal after seeing its output.

**Gate:** every recorded failure has a first-loss location and evidence. Unproven claims about strictness, source completeness and false positives remain explicitly marked as hypotheses. No code repair or paid calls in this phase.

## Phase 1: make discovery and evidence selection useful

**Purpose:** find suitable material and combine explicit contributions without inventing connections.

Inspect `lib/ask/retrieval.ts`, `tiered.ts`, `patient-models.ts`, `stable-lanes.ts`, `package.ts`, `source-writing-boundary.ts`, and the search callers in `pipeline.ts` and `lib/sources/`.

### 1A. Validate access to the existing approved library first

1. Separate material absent from the collection, material excluded by rights or source rules, material not discovered, material misclassified, and material lost in packaging.
2. Compare the configured HadeethEnc title route with the existing stored-hadith route using the same fixed questions. Establish query-language handling, authenticity, complete usable text, rights eligibility, context and result limits before any configuration switch. Existing data does not itself authorize enabling an unaccepted corpus backend.
3. Inspect scholar retrieval's approved search documents, short-excerpt limits, all-variants behavior, mapped pages and live recovery. Verify when each route actually executes. Do not equate a live-enabled flag with a live search having occurred.
4. Inspect shared ranking ceilings and source-family filtering. Verify the deployed RPC and demonstrate cross-family crowding before proposing a remedy. A required schema/RPC change is outside this refactor's no-migration boundary and needs a separate concrete proposal.
5. Inspect broad-question query construction and recovery. Retrieve broadly related candidates for review without promoting topic similarity into final claim support.
6. Audit ranking separately from selection. Review whether a zero score or a result cap removed a genuinely useful explicit contribution, qualification or background passage. Such a passage need not resolve the whole concern to remain a discovery candidate. A ranking repair requires an identified, reviewed false-zero or cap-loss example; a passage that already scored highly and reached writing is not evidence for that repair. Preserve unrelated-source controls and keep scores separate from evidence approval.

### 1B. Separate candidate assessment from complete-answer coverage

1. Track explicit support for a clear proposed statement or necessary subpoint. Allow several complementary contributions to establish a complete answer jointly. Keep related background available to explain a linked principle without using it to prove a missing conclusion.
2. Separate applicability/scope from completeness. “This passage does not explain the entire proof” may mean useful background plus a research gap; it is not automatically an audience or condition mismatch. Actual audience and condition restrictions remain binding.
3. Define rejection classes before implementation:
   - A valid but unsuitable individual candidate: keep other independently valid candidates and compute remaining gaps.
   - Empty, incomplete or contradictory assessments: attempt a bounded reassessment/contract correction on the same pool before treating it as a need for different sources. Do not infer the missing verdicts.
   - A valid missing-evidence decision: run bounded new-query recovery for the missing contribution.
   - Unknown IDs, unsafe conflicts or structurally corrupt envelopes: fail closed; do not silently discard the bad fields into a passing package.
4. Add offline controls for both outcomes: dropping one unsuitable candidate preserves a separate valid source; dropping Q4:174 from the saved assessments still does not create coverage. The repair must not fabricate the second outcome into a success.
5. Improve recovery classification without adding every error code to `patientSelectionNeedsEvidence`. Give reassessment and evidence discovery separate budgets and diagnostics within the existing aggregate limits.
6. Preserve the visitor's actual question while decomposing broad concerns. A definition or identity statement may provide relevant context for an evidence question, but cannot automatically establish its proof. Model these statement-level contributions and missing links explicitly instead of hardcoded cross-facet permissions. Keep explicit quantity, time, audience, condition and ruling requirements intact; do not invent a checklist of philosophical arguments the visitor did not request.
7. For a proposed complementary explanation, record a short support map before changing its gate: the intended statement; which original establishes each part; the necessary qualifications; any approved explanatory dependency; and the connection that remains unproved. Distinguish retaining a candidate for review, approving a source-group explanation and approving the final conclusion. Passing one boundary must not automatically pass the next.

### 1C. Design shared explanatory evidence with explicit dependencies

1. Preserve the source-stage background path that already works. Trace `explanationRequirementIds` into the actual writer and reviewer, not just the selector's output.
2. Address package construction and the writer boundary together. Removing the writer filter alone cannot give it sources absent from its independently built package.
3. Represent a required approved explanation as an evidence dependency. Recovery may seek that explanation from the relevant source family, rather than repeatedly searching only the current family. A specific dependency can delay its dependent group; it must not make every group wait for all research.
4. Do not use generated plans, provisional sibling drafts, rejected text or familiar model knowledge as evidence. Any shared explanation must retain its approved original, identity, restrictive context and source links, and be supplied consistently to the writer and the reviewer that judges its use.
5. Resolve citation policy before implementation. Under the existing single-family section rules, a statement requiring a scholar's interpretation belongs in the scholar section or final synthesis. If attributed explanatory dependencies are permitted inside a Quran/hadith section, define and validate that citation extension explicitly. Never let a scholar's meaning appear anonymously as something established by the verse alone.
6. Preserve canonical display order and immutable commits. Later discoveries cannot rewrite an already published section; do dependency research before that section is committed.

**Gate:** a documented dependency/citation contract and offline comparison demonstrate a specific useful contribution retained correctly. Complementary evidence can support the required statements jointly, while related-only evidence cannot complete an unsupported conclusion. Cross-source controls reject anonymous interpretation, missing context, provisional text and false attribution. Existing source/citation boundaries are not removed by a prompt-only change. Discovery-route readiness and any remaining corpus/schema work are recorded separately.

## Phase 2: repair writing without discarding valid work

**Purpose:** fix the failing sentence while preserving accurate, independently usable explanations.

Inspect `lib/ask/checks.ts`, `rich-engine.ts`, `patient-models.ts`, and correction-input builders reached by the active path.

1. Replay the exact copying, quotation, attribution and usefulness failures. Record the matched words or rejected clause in a private review artifact. Classify each as a genuine defect, a false positive, or unresolved.
2. Separate religious support failures from presentation problems. Do not remove a supported statement merely because a different sentence repeats it or uses awkward wording.
3. Make correction feedback identify the exact failed claim and rule. Keep unchanged, verified wording stable when its evidence and context remain identical. Recheck every changed statement and any dependent statement.
4. If a copying rule misfires, address the demonstrated pattern with positive and negative controls. Do not change arbitrary thresholds across the whole corpus. Preserve the separate display of unchanged source originals and approved translations.
5. Treat partial group publication as a deliberate contract change. A surviving claim must independently pass citation, source support, meaning, conditions, attribution and quality checks. Recheck the surviving set for pronouns, qualifications, missing antecedents, contradictions and misleading omissions before committing the group once.
6. Never salvage claims by dropping malformed required fields, unknown references, a rejected condition, or the sentence that limits another sentence's meaning. If nothing independently useful survives, keep the group unpublished.
7. Publish the group's settled surviving explanation, with a truthful limitation when needed. Whole-question coverage is assessed separately; a source group need not answer every part by itself.
8. Handle technical glosses through explicit approved linguistic/explanatory support, not a whitelist telling the checker to accept familiar religious meanings. For the saved fitrah wording, identify the exact supporting source and context before changing its verdict. A source-support rejection is not an editorial false positive merely because the phrase sounds familiar.
9. When factual checks pass but usefulness is unsure, identify what is missing: an unanswered concern, an unexplained connection, repetition, or presentation only. Use a focused quality decision or targeted edit; do not automatically approve the draft or regenerate all accurate claims. Verify that the resulting explanation is useful to the reader.
10. Keep corrections bounded under the existing policy. Each correction must name the failed claim, the supported meaning/conditions to preserve and the applicable writing rules. Compare before/after claims and failure reasons. Do not add another retry merely because a different rule failed; document the recurring interaction and repair its cause first.

**Gate:** saved supported explanations survive demonstrated editorial false positives; known copies, fabricated quotations, unsupported interpretations and lost conditions remain blocked. A targeted correction does not rewrite unrelated verified claims. The existing empty-answer cases are not declared fixed merely because one sentence can be displayed.

Required paired examples: a supported explanation remains useful after its editorial defect is repaired; a superficially similar explanation with an unsupported interpretation or missing condition remains blocked. Handwritten candidate explanations may help design these controls, but are not evidence that the real writer now succeeds.

## Phase 3: make the Haiku transport dependable

**Purpose:** prevent format complexity and output cutoffs from defeating a correct evidence path.

Inspect `lib/ai/openrouter.ts`, `structured-output.ts`, `price-ceiling.ts`, and `lib/ask/continuation-session.ts`.

1. Verify the existing supported-schema transformation and strict audit partitioning against recorded requests, including a final combined audit. Every original field and semantic obligation must remain present across the parts.
2. Check agreement between independently returned audit parts. Contradictory support, citation and condition findings must not be combined into approval. A failed part cannot yield a published partial audit.
3. Measure visible output and reasoning-token use by role from existing receipts. Distinguish frame, selection, writing, correction and checking requirements.
4. Adjust per-call allowances only from that evidence, within the unchanged aggregate token/call/spending limits. Update reservation calculations and trace metadata with the actual transmitted allowance. Do not silently reduce review effort or add automatic paid retries.
5. Preserve configured backups and make their use visible in the private report. A mixed-model answer must not be reported as a pure Haiku result.
6. Treat raising a role's output allowance, reducing review scope, disabling reasoning and adding a retry as different interventions. Select a measured allowance change first if warranted; do not bundle these changes or silently switch review effort.

**Gate:** offline tests cover strict-part completeness, invalid parts, contradictory findings, cancellation, output truncation and accurate reservation accounting. TypeScript passes. Live compatibility is still pending until Phase 6; saved-response tests cannot prove future model compliance.

## Phase 4: make failures and continuation honest

**Purpose:** show where research stopped and resume only work that can safely progress.

1. Retain the first decisive reason alongside the final result. Distinguish no candidates, insufficient relevant evidence, invalid selection, rejected draft, unsupported claim, editorial correction failure, provider rejection, output truncation, reservation refusal, unknown charge and final coverage gap.
2. Distinguish a trusted refusal before dispatch from a sent request with an unknown charge. The former must not invent an unpaid provider receipt; the latter must stay held or fully reserved under an explicitly approved operator policy.
3. Keep diagnostics free of visitor wording. Detailed private review artifacts are separate from ordinary logs and reader messages.
4. Continue must preserve committed sections and resume unfinished work within the same remaining limits. Do not offer it for a locked integrity failure or perform whole-answer restarts.
5. Give a short localized final status beneath the available answer. Do not say displayed evidence passed if nothing is displayed. Do not show a premature “incomplete answer” while research is still active.
6. Connect recovery statuses to Phase 1B: unsuitable candidate, assessment repair, missing-source recovery and unsafe contract failure must remain distinguishable. A malformed selector response must not look like a search that found nothing.

**Gate:** offline route tests distinguish content failure, pre-dispatch refusal, unknown billing, cancellation and missing final coverage. Continue either advances eligible unfinished work or gives an accurate unavailable reason without a paid restart.

## Phase 5: verify the stable reader against repaired publications

**Purpose:** protect the approved experience while backend behavior changes.

- Reveal only settled, checked groups in canonical order. Keep later ready groups buffered until their turn.
- Keep published sections mounted through later research, reconnect, Continue and a failed conclusion. Exact duplicate delivery is harmless; conflicting replacements fail.
- Genuine late findings retain the container and hide only affected content with an integrity notice.
- Preserve Arabic-aware checked-text reveal, reduced motion, Show full text, links, attribution, source accents and Report Problem.
- Verify Stop and reading position. Scrolling upward pauses auto-follow; Jump to latest restores it. Phone layout and Arabic RTL must remain usable.
- Replay explanatory dependencies arriving before commit, unavailable dependencies, integrity changes in shared originals, and later research that would prefer different wording. None may expose an unchecked explanation or rewrite a committed group.

**Gate:** focused offline reader/route replays cover all these cases with network calls forbidden. Review the actual reader visually using clearly labeled fixtures. This gate does not count as a successful live answer.

## Phase 6: prove useful answers in the real browser

**Purpose:** decide whether the feature works for readers.

1. Run the relevant source-integrity checks, TypeScript and build after the implementation phases. Repeat checks only when a new change or failure justifies it.
2. Reconcile or conservatively reserve old unknown costs under the existing approved policy. Use only the remaining explicitly approved allowance; if a run cannot fit its reservations, record the test as allowance-limited.
3. Lock the test set and evaluation criteria before paid calls. Run a bounded English and Arabic sample, including the previously failed cases. Do not rerun repeatedly until favorable output appears or replace a failed question with an easier one.
4. Watch the real UI from submission to completion. Record time to first useful checked explanation, section order and retention, actual model/fallback use, final status, calls and cost. Measure time without making it a research deadline.
5. Read each shown explanation and conclusion against the cited originals and necessary context. Passing an automated checker is not religious-content acceptance.
6. Report results per question: useful and source-supported; safely limited with a useful explanation; justified refusal; or failed. A spinner followed by an empty answer is a failure on an answerable case.
7. Publish a before/after result table for the same locked cases, including unanswered concerns and limitations. Report discovery retention, checked useful claims, complete answers, justified refusals, content failures and provider/allowance interruptions separately. Do not substitute “more candidates reached writing” for successful answers or claim percentage improvements without a defined denominator.
8. Apply a reader-facing usefulness rubric fixed in Phase 0: the explanation addresses a real part of the question, explains the cited teaching in understandable language, and identifies any material unanswered concern. A generic related passage, an unexplained quotation or a disclaimer cannot turn an empty or evasive answer into a successful limited answer. Retain failed cases in the report and diagnose their earliest unresolved failure before another repair.

**Acceptance gate:** the locked answerable set produces useful cited explanations, the negative controls withhold unsupported claims, no committed group disappears, and every conclusion stays within verified evidence. No known critical source-integrity defect may remain. A source-card-only result, component-test pass, pre-recorded browser replay, or spending-truncated run cannot establish complete acceptance. Record any uncompleted Stop/reconnect/save or content-review checks explicitly. Production rollout needs its own decision after this gate.
