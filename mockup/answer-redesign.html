From the Quran

Quran

The Quran states that humanity stands in constant need of God, whereas God is entirely self-sufficient and free of any need. Quran 35:15

The Quran explains that the sole purpose for the creation of jinn and mankind is to worship Him. Quran 51:56

The Quran highlights that when people are called to spend or act for God's cause, any reluctance or withholding harms only themselves, as God remains completely independent of human wealth or actions. Quran 47:38

From the Sunnah

Hadith

The Prophet reported that Allah stated humanity can neither harm nor benefit Him. Muslim 2577

Allah explains that if all creation were equally pious, it would not increase His dominion. Muslim 2577

Allah records human deeds to fully recompense people for them. Muslim 2577

Muslim 2577 Hadith Qudsi
Read source ↗
صحيح · رواه مسلم

Abu Dharr (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: The Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him), as part of what he narrated from his Lord, said that Allah Almighty said: "O My slaves, I have made oppression forbidden for Myself and have made it forbidden amongst you, so do not oppress one another... were the first of you and the last of you to become as pious as the most pious heart among you, that would not increase My kingdom in anything..."

Original passage and translation
عَنْ أَبِي ذَرٍّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ فِيمَا رَوَى عَنِ اللَّهِ تَبَارَكَ وَتَعَالَى، أَنَّهُ قَالَ: «يَا عِبَادِي إِنِّي حَرَّمْتُ الظُّلْمَ عَلَى نَفْسِي...»

Abu Dharr narrated: "O My servants, indeed I have forbidden oppression for Myself..."

Bayan's summary

God is entirely self-sufficient and independent, needing nothing from His creation. Quran 35:15 Quran 47:38

Humanity was created to worship Allah, yet this worship does not increase His dominion or benefit Him. Quran 51:56 Muslim 2577

People stand in absolute need of God for guidance and sustenance, and their deeds are recorded for their own ultimate recompense. Quran 35:15 Muslim 2577
