Does touching something impure automatically break one’s ablution (Wudu)?
Verified Scholarly Consensus (Rajih)

Touching an impurity does not invalidate Wudu, though washing the affected spot is obligatory before praying.

According to the verified ruling of the majority of scholars, merely coming into contact with an impurity (Najasah) does not break one’s state of ritual purity (Wudu). Quran 5:6 The nullifiers of Wudu are explicitly defined in the Sunnah and do not include physical contact with impure substances. Bukhari 137

However, one must wash the impurity off the skin or garment before offering prayer (Salah), as purity of body, clothing, and place is a strict precondition for prayer validity. Ibn Baz (Majmu’ 10/140)

Prophetic Sunnah — Sahih al-Bukhari #137 Authentic (Sahih)
Narrated Abu Huraira (may Allah be pleased with him): The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said:
“The prayer of a person who does Hadath (passes wind, urine, or stool) will not be accepted until he performs ablution.”
Approved Scholar Commentary
IB
Shaykh 'Abd al-'Aziz ibn Baz Majmu' Fatawa wa Maqalat Mutanawwi'ah (10/140)
“Touching impurity does not break the wudu; rather, it is only incumbent upon the person who touched it to wash the location that was soiled, whether hand or foot. As for his wudu, it remains intact and valid.”
