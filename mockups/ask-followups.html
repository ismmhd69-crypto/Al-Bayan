Can a woman travel without a mahram?

حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، وَزُهَيْرُ بْنُ حَرْبٍ، كِلاَهُمَا عَنْ سُفْيَانَ، - قَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ بْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ، - حَدَّثَنَا عَمْرُو بْنُ دِينَارٍ، عَنْ أَبِي مَعْبَدٍ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ ابْنَ عَبَّاسٍ، يَقُولُ سَمِعْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَخْطُبُ يَقُولُ ‏"‏ لاَ يَخْلُوَنَّ رَجُلٌ بِامْرَأَةٍ إِلاَّ وَمَعَهَا ذُو مَحْرَمٍ وَلاَ تُسَافِرِ الْمَرْأَةُ إِلاَّ مَعَ ذِي مَحْرَمٍ ‏"‏ ‏.‏ فَقَامَ رَجُلٌ فَقَالَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ إِنَّ امْرَأَتِي خَرَجَتْ حَاجَّةً وَإِنِّي اكْتُتِبْتُ فِي غَزْوَةِ كَذَا وَكَذَا ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏"‏ انْطَلِقْ فَحُجَّ مَعَ امْرَأَتِكَ ‏"‏ ‏.‏

Abu Bakr ibn Abi Shaybah and Zuhayr ibn Harb narrated to us, both from Sufyan. Abu Bakr said: Sufyan ibn Uyaynah narrated to us. Amr ibn Dinar narrated to us, from Abu Ma'bad. He said: I heard Ibn Abbas say: I heard the Prophet (peace be upon him) deliver a sermon saying: No man should be alone with a woman unless she has a mahram with her, and a woman should not travel except with a mahram. A man stood and said: Messenger of Allah, my wife has gone out for Hajj, while I have been enlisted for such-and-such expedition. He said: Go and perform Hajj with your wife.

Scholars' explanations

Ibn BazRead source ↗

As for a woman traveling without a mahram, this is not permissible. The Lawgiver has stipulated this, and the Prophet (peace be upon him) has stipulated the prohibition of this. He said in the authentic hadith: 'A woman shall not travel except with a mahram.' When a man informed him that his wife had gone out for Hajj, and he had already signed up for some military expeditions, he said to him: 'Go back and perform Hajj with your wife.' So he sent him back from the expedition and ordered him to go with his wife for Hajj. The point is: her traveling without a mahram is not permissible, neither by plane, nor by car, nor by train, nor otherwise. As for her ordinary exit from her house to school, or to the market, or to such and such, it is likewise not permissible for her to be alone with a non-mahram (stranger).

what if she goes to hajj with a group of women?
Follow-up: searched for this new part
Ibn BazRead source ↗

الصواب أنهم يأثمون إلا بمحرم؛ لقول النبي ﷺ: لا تسافر المرأة إلا مع ذي محرم، وهو يعم سفر الحج وغيره. وليس على المرأة حج إذا لم تجد محرمًا يسافر معها، وقد رخص بعض العلماء في ذلك إذا كانت مع جماعة من النساء بصحبة رجال مؤمنين ولكن ليس عليه دليل، والصواب خلافه للحديث المذكور.

The correct view is that they commit a sin except with a mahram, due to the statement of the Prophet (peace be upon him): A woman must not travel except with a mahram. This encompasses Hajj travel and other travel. A woman is not obligated to perform Hajj if she does not find a mahram to travel with her. Some scholars have granted a concession in this regard if she is with a group of women in the company of believing men, but there is no evidence for it, and the correct view is contrary to it due to the aforementioned hadith.
