Who is Allah?

The first question of faith: understanding the Creator – not through images, but through what He says about Himself.

Jump to quiz

The clear answer

The Quran describes Allah as the One: He was not born, has no offspring, and none is like Him. This is where the Islamic concept of God begins. Allah is not a member of a family of gods. Quran 112

He is the Creator of all things. Creator and creation are distinct: the One who is worshipped does not depend on those who worship Him. Quran 39:6235:15

He knows the unseen and the seen, and is merciful. His oneness does not cancel these descriptions. Quran 59:2242:11

Understanding together

Bayan’s explanation of the verses cited below each point.

When we meet a person, questions about parents and birth make sense, because that person belongs to creation. The Islamic teaching about Allah starts with a different distinction: He is the Creator, not another being within creation. Source: Quran 39:62, Quran 42:11

Three meanings help to make sense of this:

Oneness

Allah has no partner and no counterpart.Quran 112:1–4

Independence

He does not need creation – people need Him.Quran 35:15, 112:2

Mercy

Kindness toward people is part of how He is described.Quran 1:3, 7:156
