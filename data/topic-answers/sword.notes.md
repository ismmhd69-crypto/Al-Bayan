# Was Islam spread by the sword?

I read the three questions from `public.topic_texts`. I searched and fetched Quran Foundation passages for compulsion, belief, fighting those who fight, kindness to non-combatants, peace and protection. I read the surrounding verses for 2:190, 2:256, 8:61, 9:6, 10:99 and 60:8 before selecting the direct evidence.

The selected sources are Quran 2:256, 10:99, 2:190, 8:61, 60:8 and 9:6. They directly state no compulsion, reject compelling belief, limit the selected fighting command to those who fight, require peace when the other side inclines to it, allow kindness and justice toward non-combatants, and require safety for a person seeking protection.

No hadith or scholar quote was needed. I did not turn these verses into a complete claim about every historical conquest or conversion. The limitation note is unsourced and identifies that historical gap honestly.
