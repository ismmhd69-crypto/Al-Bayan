# When were hadith written?

I read the three questions from `public.topic_texts`. I searched the HadeethEnc catalogue through `lib/sources/hadith.ts` for writing, Abu Shah, and the prohibition on writing. I fetched the two selected records through the same module and checked the Sahihayn references and grades. The HadeethEnc pages confirm the printed numbers used here: Sahih al-Bukhari 2434 and Sahih Muslim 1355 for HE6637, and Sahih Muslim 3004 for HE10849.

HE6637 is marked `متفق عليه` and records the Prophet ordering that Abu Shah's requested statement be written. HE10849 is marked `رواه مسلم` and records the wording that nothing from the Prophet other than the Quran should be written, while narration from him was allowed. HadeethEnc serves no German translation for these two records, so `translation_de` is null and the German answer is a source-based paraphrase of the Arabic and English records.

I did not use the HadeethEnc explanations as scholar opinions. I used only the hadith text, collection, printed number, grade and attribution. No scholar quote or Quran verse was needed. The limitation note is unsourced and limits the conclusion to what these two reports establish.
