## Research

I read the three questions from `public.topic_texts`. Quran evidence was searched and fetched only through `lib/sources/quran.ts`. I read the surrounding passages 2:154-157 and 67:1-3 before using 2:155-157 and 67:2.

The hadith search through `lib/sources/hadith.ts` found HE3701, Sahih al-Bukhari 5641 and Sahih Muslim 2572, graded sahih and marked muttafaq alayh. It directly covers fatigue, illness, sorrow, harm and distress. HE65772 and HE3298 were also found, but they were not needed because HE3701 answered the direct point more precisely.

The scholar library contained Ibn Baz fatwa 13937, source `S5c7e615c-f05b-48b3-8a49-76335cdcb083`. I fetched and read the complete official page. The answer distinguishes illness and similar harm from death or the death of a relative, and also mentions repentance when a calamity follows wrongdoing. The stored quote is unchanged and below 600 characters.
