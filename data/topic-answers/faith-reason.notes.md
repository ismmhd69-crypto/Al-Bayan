# Faith and reason research notes

## Research

The three questions ask whether Islam requires blind belief. I searched and fetched Quran evidence only through `lib/sources/quran.ts`. I read the contexts 2:163-165, 3:189-192, and 52:34-36 before using 2:164, 3:190-191, and 52:35-36.

The strongest direct evidence was Quranic: signs are presented for people who use their minds, reflection on creation is described, and the Quran asks whether people were created without an agent or created themselves. No hadith or scholar quote was needed for the direct answer, so none was added to the source package.
